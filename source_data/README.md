# Figure 2 Source Data

These tab-separated files contain the plotted coordinates and reciprocal-overlap calculations for the independent `Figure2a`, `Figure2b`, and `Figure2c` panel exports. They are generated by `../plot_figure2.R` from the processed BEDPE and narrowPeak files.

- `fig2a_mean_iPET_by_replicate_and_10kb_bin.tsv`: mean iPET support for each replicate and 10-kb interaction-span bin.
- `fig2a_mean_iPET_by_condition_and_10kb_bin.tsv`: replicate-averaged bin values for each genotype and cell state.
- `fig2a_loess_curve_coordinates.tsv`: coordinates of the LOESS curves displayed in panel a.
- `fig2b_peak_reciprocal_overlap.tsv`: directional peak-overlap percentages and the median across the two directions for each genotype and cell state.
- `fig2c_interaction_reciprocal_overlap.tsv`: directional two-anchor interaction-overlap percentages and the median across the two directions for each genotype and cell state.

The median of two directional percentages is their arithmetic mean. Percentages are retained at full precision in these files; labels in panels b and c are rounded to one decimal place.
